Privacy Policy — Seller Helper AI for eBay

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Seller Helper AI for eBay ("the app") helps you photograph a secondhand item, analyze it with AI, and publish it as a listing on your own eBay account. This policy describes what data the app handles and where it goes. The app is developed by an independent developer and is not affiliated with or endorsed by eBay Inc.

Information the app collects

  • Google account (sign-in): if you sign in, we receive your Google account email address and identifier via Firebase Authentication. This identifies your token balance (tokens are used to publish listings).
  • Photos: photos you take or pick are stored on your device. They are sent to Google's Gemini AI service (via Firebase) to analyze the item, and uploaded to eBay's picture hosting service when you publish a listing.
  • eBay account connection: when you connect your eBay account, eBay issues the app access tokens that are stored only on your device (they are excluded from device backups). The app also stores your eBay user ID on your device so Settings can show which eBay account is connected. The app uses the tokens to create listings on your behalf when you explicitly tap Publish — never on its own.
  • Listing details: item details (title, description, item specifics, condition, price), your ZIP code, and package weight and dimensions are stored on your device and sent to eBay as part of the listings you publish. The item's title and description may also be sent to Gemini to fill in item details eBay asks for at publish time.
  • Token balance and spend records: your token balance and per-listing spend records are stored in Google Firestore, tied to your signed-in account.
  • Purchases: token packs are bought through Google Play, which handles payment — the app never sees your payment details. To credit your tokens, the app's server verifies the purchase with Google Play and keeps a record of it (product, number of tokens, Google Play order ID, and your account identifier).
  • Feedback: if you choose to send feedback from the app, we store your star rating (1–5), your optional comment (up to 200 characters), the app version, and the time, tied to your signed-in account (email address and identifier) so the developer can follow up. Feedback is only sent when you tap Send.
  • App integrity and configuration: the app uses Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity) to confirm requests come from the genuine app, and Firebase Remote Config to receive settings such as the AI model name. These services use a Firebase installation identifier for this purpose only.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with third parties beyond the services named above (Google Firebase, Google Play, and eBay), which are used solely to provide the app's functionality.
  • No background activity on your eBay account — listings are created only on your explicit action.
  • No eBay account data is stored on our servers.

Data retention and deletion

Item entries and photos live on your device and are deleted when you delete them or uninstall the app. Disconnecting your eBay account removes its tokens and user ID from your device. To delete your account data on our servers (sign-in record, token balance, spend history, and feedback you sent), follow the steps on the account deletion page. Purchase records may be kept as needed for accounting, tax, and refund purposes.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and requires an eBay account to publish. It is not directed at children.
